# Vakhta

## Product and landing page plan

Sales Manager handoff • English edition • 18 September 2026

Vakhta is a platform for organizing the work of shift teams. It connects schedules, attendance, issue reporting, checklists, workplace handover and reporting. Workers use Telegram and a QR kiosk; managers use a web panel.

This document supports presentations to business owners and investors and gives the delivery team a landing-page brief. The page has three conversion paths: request a demonstration, discuss a business pilot and discuss investment.

### Mission

Help people on shift respond to problems in time and hand over work with a clear understanding of workplace conditions and responsibility.

### The central problem

When the schedule lives in a spreadsheet, issues are discussed in chat, and photos and decisions are stored separately, managers must reconstruct what happened. The next shift may not know what remains unresolved. Vakhta connects those records to the work and the people responsible for decisions.

### Who it serves

• Manufacturing operations with shift teams are the primary product context. Buyers include owners, operations directors and production heads. Users include workers, shift masters, planners, HR, inspectors and accounting.
• Hotels are a potential use case for housekeeping and service teams: zone readiness, photo evidence and work handover. Process fit requires validation; reservations and room inventory are not claimed.
• Warehouses, cleaning and facility-service teams are potential use cases for shifts, inspections and zone handover. Inventory management and dispatch are not claimed.

### Value for each participant

Owners gain connected records for investigating operational problems. Shift masters see matters requiring a decision and retain its context. Workers have a clear channel to report issues and hand over work. HR and accounting work with recorded facts within their roles.

### Positioning boundaries

Vakhta does not replace ERP, MES, PMS, WMS or payroll systems. Time reports are not OEE or a finding of employee fault. Lower downtime and operating costs are outcomes to measure in a pilot, not guarantees to make beforehand.

# Landing page copy and structure

### Opening section

**Shift work with clear actions and responsibility.**
Vakhta connects schedules, attendance, issue reporting and workplace handover. Workers use Telegram, managers use one web panel, and a QR kiosk records arrival and departure.

Buttons: **Request a demo** · **Discuss a pilot** · **Discuss investment**.
Reading link: **See how a shift works**.

### The problem

**Scattered records make it harder to understand the whole situation.**
A chat message does not always become an issue someone handles. A photo without context does not explain workplace conditions. Verbal handover leaves room for misunderstanding. Vakhta connects messages, evidence and decisions to the work they concern.

### How it works

1. Plan the shift and publish the schedule for employees.
2. Record arrival through QR and start work through Telegram.
3. Run the shift, report issues and bring them to the responsible manager.
4. Complete the checklist and hand over the zone with photos and recorded remarks.
5. Review reports and decision history to understand events and determine the next actions.

### Teams

**Starting with manufacturing. Checking the fit for your process.**
Vakhta is being developed for shift-based operational work. If you manage a hotel, warehouse or service team, show us your handover process. Together, we can establish what fits today and what would require adaptation.

### Demonstration and trust

**See the complete journey from schedule to workplace handover.**
The demonstration connects the working scenarios. Responsible people retain decision authority; photos, remarks and history help them make informed decisions.

### Pilot

**Start with one process whose results can be checked.**
Agree on the team, zone, accountable people and success criteria. After the pilot, compare results with the baseline and determine the next step.

### Investors

**Building a platform for managing shift-team operations.**
The initial focus is manufacturing. Expansion into other industries is tested against their actual workflows. We invite a discussion about the product, strategy and evidence needed to scale.

### Closing action

**Choose your next step: a demonstration, a pilot or an investment discussion.**
For a demonstration, tell us about your team; for a pilot, describe the process you want to test; for an investment conversation, outline your interest in the product. The owner must confirm contact details before publication.

# Capabilities and common questions

### Capability catalog for the page

• **Schedules.** Plan and publish shifts so the team knows when and where to work.
• **QR and attendance.** Record arrival and departure; a missing record does not become an invented fact.
• **Shift execution.** Record work, breaks, service time and downtime in the context of the shift.
• **Incidents.** Report problems, handle them and escalate them according to configured rules.
• **Checklists.** Role- and context-based checks, remarks and photographs.
• **Zone handover.** Recorded workplace conditions, remarks and decisions by the responsible person.
• **Employee requests.** Vacation, days off, sick leave and other supported requests with decision routes.
• **Communications.** Messages and questionnaires for selected employees, with sending and response states kept separate.
• **Bonus scores.** Calculations from rules and recorded facts, with adjustment history. This is not payroll.
• **Reports and audit.** Recorded-time accounting, exports and action history for investigating events.
• **Photo review assistance.** AI can suggest findings; a person reviews and decides. No accuracy or automatic-acceptance promise is made.

### Does everyone need a separate application

Workers use Telegram; managers open the web panel. QR attendance requires a suitable device at the entrance. Check whether Telegram and devices are permitted at the workplace.

### Does the product work without internet

This material does not claim a verified offline workflow. Check connectivity and phone-use rules before a pilot.

### Can it connect to our ERP or hotel system

This material does not claim ready-made ERP, MES, PMS, WMS or payroll integrations. Integration needs and scope require a separate discussion.

### Does AI replace the shift master

No. Photo assistance supports human review. The broader Master-agent concept is a development direction, not a promise of autonomous operational decisions.

### What does it cost and how long does rollout take

Terms follow qualification of the team, sites, required workflows and support. This document establishes no public price, free pilot or fixed rollout period.

### Is it suitable for a hotel

There is potential fit for shift-based service teams and zone-condition handover. Validate it against a specific process; Vakhta is not presented as a reservations or hotel-management system.

# Presenting to a business owner

### Approximately one minute opening

“Vakhta helps organize a shift team's work, from schedules and attendance to issue reporting and workplace handover. Workers interact through Telegram, while managers work with connected records in the panel. The aim is to understand work and decisions in context, without having to reconstruct events from separate chats and spreadsheets. I suggest selecting one of your processes and showing it in a demonstration. If the fit is right, we can agree on a bounded pilot and test the results against your measures.”

### Qualification questions

1. How are shifts, teams and zones organized? Who is accountable for handing over work?
2. Where do schedules, issue reports, photos and decisions live today?
3. Which problem keeps recurring, and how do you currently measure its consequences?
4. Are Telegram, phones and photography permitted? What is connectivity like in work areas?
5. Who decides on a pilot, budget and data use?
6. Which process could be tested separately without a mandatory integration with other systems?

### A 10 to 15 minute demonstration

**Context:** ask the buyer to identify one problem. **Schedule:** show the shift plan.
**Execution:** show arrival and shift state using demo records. **Issue:** show the incident,
responsible person and handling history. **Handover:** show a checklist, photos and a human decision.
**Close:** open the report and agree on the next step. Describe a hotel example as process adaptation,
not as a customer case study.

### Pilot proposal

One team or zone, one accountable person from the customer, an agreed duration and a defined set of
scenarios. Before starting, establish the baseline, support terms, data access and continuation
criteria. Do not promise a paid or free pilot before commercial terms are agreed.

• **Response to issues:** median time from a recorded report to the first recorded action; compare equivalent issue categories before and after.
• **Handover completeness:** complete reports divided by all reports due; count missing reports separately.
• **Manager effort:** active minutes spent preparing and reviewing, using the same measurement method.
• **Adoption:** workers completing the required scenario divided by all pilot participants; separately record refusals and access problems.

Agree on targets before starting. Missing data is not zero; account for concurrent process changes.

### Concise objection handling

**“We already have chat and Excel.”** Let us identify where you manually connect records and reconstruct decisions.
**“People will not use it.”** Test one scenario under actual access and training conditions.
**“Show us the savings.”** Agree on baseline measurements and pilot criteria; do not invent a percentage.

# Presenting to an investor

### Product thesis

Vakhta connects shift workers' actions and managers' decisions in a shared operational context.
Manufacturing is the initial application segment. Expansion to other shift teams depends on
repeatable workflows, validated demand and the cost of implementation.

### Approximately ninety second opening

“Vakhta addresses an operational problem for shift teams: schedules, messages, photographs and decisions are often split across separate tools. We connect them around the working shift, using Telegram for workers and a web panel for managers. The product covers planning, attendance, incidents, photo-backed handover and reporting. We start with manufacturing; hotels, warehouses and service teams are expansion hypotheses. The next commercial task is to validate repeatable demand, measurable value and a viable implementation cost. I suggest demonstrating the product and then discussing the evidence needed for an investment decision.”

### Conversation sequence

1. A specific team's problem and how it is handled today.
2. A complete operational workflow demonstration, including human accountability.
3. The initial segment, buyer and sales-channel hypothesis.
4. Verified pilot results, once prepared and cleared for disclosure.
5. Revenue model, implementation/support costs and a plan to test assumptions.
6. Funding amount, instrument and milestones, following a separate owner decision.

### Evidence to prepare for a substantive discussion

• Customers and pilots: counts, status, dates and permission to name them; test records are not customers.
• Revenue: separate paid and contracted revenue, with period, currency and supporting source.
• Usage and retention: definitions of activity, cohorts and observation windows.
• Economics: pricing and costs of setup, support, infrastructure and AI; separate actuals from forecasts.
• Market: segment, geography, sources and calculation method; no unverified TAM on the landing page.
• Team, product ownership, funding terms and specific milestones that capital would finance.

### Monetization hypothesis

A subscription and a separate implementation service are options to test. The billing unit, price
and terms should follow buyer conversations. This is neither an active price list nor a financial forecast.

### Risks and development direction

Test dependence on Telegram, device access, implementation in new industries, willingness to pay
and support costs. Deeper AI assistance and a Master agent are development directions. Autonomous
decisions about people, safety, time and bonuses are not a current promise.

# Specification and delivery plan

### First version scope

One public landing experience with three complete language editions. Section order: opening →
problem → workflow → capabilities → audience → demonstration → pilot → trust → investors → FAQ →
contact. All three actions are visible in the opening and contact sections. Contact links carry
separate demo, pilot and investor context; opening a link does not mean a request has been sent.

### Language and accessibility requirements

Separate /uk/, /en/ and /ru/ URLs, equivalent content, a visible switcher and preservation of the
current section when switching language. Translate buttons, captions, alternative text, search
metadata and sharing text too. Do not force browser-language redirects. Root offers a language choice.

Provide a readable mobile layout, keyboard navigation, visible focus and sufficient contrast. Core
copy, language links, FAQ and contact work without JavaScript. Narrow viewports and enlarged text
must not clip content. Published images have permission and contain no personal data.

### Stages and owners

1. **Owner and Sales:** confirm contacts, site operator, domain, commercial wording and asset permissions.
2. **Sales and product:** review the three editions, prepare demo records and verify every demonstration scenario.
3. **Designer:** create phone and desktop structure/layouts and accommodate longer translations.
4. **Developer:** build a separate public site within the product's existing ecosystem, with locale pages, metadata and contact navigation.
5. **QA and Sales:** check all languages, links, mobile layout, keyboard access and factual claims; rehearse the presentation.
6. **Release owner:** publish, check live URLs and preserve a route back to the previous version.
7. **Sales and owner:** collect qualified conversations and pilot outcomes, and update material from evidence.

### Acceptance criteria

• Readers can explain whom the product serves, its central problem and a complete shift workflow.
• All three language editions have equivalent sections and working contacts for all three intents.
• Every capability claim has a source and a verified demonstration scenario before publication.
• Screenshots of every section on phone and desktop are captured and visually inspected in each language.
• No invented customers, results, prices, integrations or AI-autonomy claims appear.
• Sales can deliver customer and investor presentations and record the next step.

### Decisions before publication

Real contact details, operator identity, domain, authorized imagery and agreed commercial terms are
required. Estimate timing and budget after these decisions. This document does not implement the
site, a lead form, CRM, payments or new product capabilities. The current delivery is the specification,
plan and presentation material.
