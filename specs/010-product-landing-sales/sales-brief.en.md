# Vakhta for manufacturing

## Landing page and sales guide

Sales Manager handoff • 18 September 2026

Vakhta helps manufacturers investigate recorded time loss, respond to incidents and organize personnel checks and shift handover. Workers report through Telegram; responsible managers handle issues in the web panel. The commercial task is to test whether manufacturers need this workflow and are willing to commit to a bounded pilot.

### Mission

Help production teams find recurring causes of time loss and act on them, with the aim of reducing avoidable downtime. Personnel checklists and clear handover help retain the context of problems between shifts.

### The problem we test

A worker notices a problem, but the responsible person learns about it late. Actions are discussed in chat, checks are missed and the next shift inherits unresolved issues. Managers lack connected records to understand which loss reasons keep recurring. Sales must establish whether this problem actually matters to each manufacturer.

### Who the page serves

Manufacturing owners, operations directors and production heads are the buyers. Shift masters are daily champions; workers and reviewers participate in reporting, checks and handover. Qualification starts with the production process and a recent incident. The current campaign covers manufacturing only.

### Product and outcome boundaries

Vakhta connects recorded worker activity, incidents, checklists, photos, handover and reports. These records support investigation. They do not automatically identify machine bottlenecks, establish root cause or calculate OEE. Employee downtime is not machine downtime; overlapping employee intervals must not be summed into equipment stop duration.

Less downtime and faster response are intended outcomes to measure. No percentage saving, verified customer demand, fixed rollout period or automatic equipment monitoring is promised.

### Three next steps

**Discuss a pilot** is the strongest action. **Request a demo** offers a lower commitment. **Discuss investment** remains visible but secondary. Investor interest is recorded separately from manufacturing customer demand.

# Capabilities and buyer questions

### Core capabilities to demonstrate

• Recorded activity and time-loss reports provide reasons and context for investigation.
• Incidents connect reporting, handling and configured escalation.
• Personnel checklists capture completion, remarks and photographs.
• Zone handover retains workplace conditions and human review decisions.

### Supporting capabilities

Schedules and QR attendance connect work to the shift. Employee requests and communications support team coordination. Rule-based bonus scores retain adjustment history; they are not payroll. Reports, exports and audit preserve recorded facts. Photo review assistance may suggest findings, while people review and decide. Rehearse advertised scenarios at the selected release before publication.

### Does Vakhta detect equipment stoppages automatically

This offer does not include automatic machine telemetry, OEE or production-line capacity analysis. Recorded losses help direct investigation. Actual equipment downtime requires the factory's independent event data and an agreed measurement method.

### Can we create checks for our personnel

Demonstrate supported checklist configuration, required steps, remarks, photos and review using a real manufacturing scenario with demo data. Agree the necessary workflow before a pilot; do not promise an unverified configuration.

### What does the team need

Workers use Telegram and managers use the web panel; QR attendance uses a suitable entrance device. Verify connectivity, phone and photography rules. No verified offline workflow is promised.

### Does it integrate with our systems

Ready-made ERP, MES, equipment and payroll integrations are not part of this offer. If an integration is mandatory, establish it before agreeing that a pilot is feasible.

### Who decides and what does it cost

Responsible people retain operational decisions. AI assistance does not replace the shift master. Price, setup, support and pilot terms follow qualification; there is no implied free pilot or published price list.

# Sales conversation and demand test

### One minute opening

“Where does your production lose time, and how quickly does the right person learn about a problem? Vakhta connects worker reports, incident handling, personnel checks and shift handover. The aim is to investigate recurring losses and reduce avoidable downtime. Let us take one recent incident, show the relevant workflow and decide whether a bounded pilot is worth testing.”

### Qualification and demonstration

Ask about a recent interruption, its consequences, the current workaround, the decision maker and the next step. Check access, connectivity, the pilot owner and mandatory integrations. Demonstrate issue → responsible response → checklist and handover → loss report in 10 to 15 minutes with authorized demo records.

### Predeclare the experiment

Before collecting campaign outcomes, Sales and the owner record the manufacturing cohort, acquisition channel, observation window, review date and continue/adapt/stop thresholds. No numeric targets are invented here. Record them before reviewing results.

A qualified conversation records company and process, decision role, recurring problem, current workaround and an agreed next step. Deduplicate companies. Record objections, refusals and no-fit reasons alongside positive evidence.

### Evidence of need and commitment

Separate known exposure, contacts received, qualified conversations, held demos, agreed pilots, started pilots and willingness to discuss payment. A pilot commitment names an accountable person and a concrete next step with time/data access under discussion. Compliments, clicks, investor interest and internal tests are not manufacturing demand.

Use a manual experiment sheet. Without a reliable exposure denominator, do not calculate conversion rates. At the agreed review, decide **continue, adapt, stop or inconclusive**. Low reach or missing evidence cannot prove absence of need.

### Objections and close

**“We have chat and spreadsheets.”** Explore one delayed response or recurring problem and its current workaround. **“People will not use it.”** Test one scenario under actual workplace constraints. **“Show the savings.”** Agree a baseline and measurement before promising a result.

Close with a named next step, responsible person and agreed date. This planning package does not authorize prospect outreach.

# Pilot evidence and delivery plan

### Test effectiveness separately

One production process, a customer owner, agreed duration, support and commercial terms. Define comparable baseline and pilot scopes before starting. Measure report-to-first-recorded-action time, repeated incident reasons, checklist completeness, manager effort and adoption. State denominators and missing-data rules; account for other process changes.

Measure actual machine downtime only from independently established factory events. Do not sum employees' overlapping downtime. A commitment to a pilot is demand evidence; reduced downtime requires measured pilot results.

### Investor conversation

“Vakhta tests a manufacturing need: connecting reported problems, response and shift checks so managers can investigate recurring losses. The next milestone is evidence of buyer commitment and measurable pilot value. We invite a discussion about the product, implementation costs and the evidence required for an investment decision.”

Prepare dated, permissioned customer/pilot counts, revenue, usage, costs and retention only when available. Keep forecasts separate. Market size, funding amount, instrument and use of funds require evidence and an owner decision. A subscription and implementation service are monetization hypotheses, not an active offer.
