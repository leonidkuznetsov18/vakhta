## Accepted demand protocol

The project owner keeps the manual evidence record.

- Cohort: 20 independent manufacturing companies with shift work.
- Channel: personal Sales contacts. This implementation does not authorize or perform outreach.
- Window: four weeks from the recorded campaign start; the owner sets the start and review dates
  before first outreach. Website publication alone does not start the campaign.
- Continue: at least five qualified conversations and two agreed pilots.
- Adapt: recurring problems are confirmed but commitments do not meet the continue threshold.
- Stop this proposition: fewer than three confirmed recurring problems after 20 substantive contacts.
- Inconclusive: insufficient reach, unless the continue threshold is already met.

A qualified conversation records company/process, buying role, a recent recurring problem, current
workaround and agreed next step. A pilot names an accountable person and a concrete next step with
time/data access and commercial terms under discussion. Deduplicate by company.

Separate known exposure, contacts, qualified conversations, demos held, pilots agreed/started and
willingness to discuss payment. Record objections, refusals and poor fit. Investor and test activity
are separate. Unknown exposure stays unknown; do not infer conversion rates or savings from clicks.
