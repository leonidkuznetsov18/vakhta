# Vakhta organization

The organization directory distinguishes groups of employees from places of responsibility.

## Language

**Unit** (Ukrainian: Підрозділ; Russian: Подразделение):
An organizational group with employee assignments and schedules. A unit can contain several zones
and can have a parent unit. A workshop can be a unit; units also include non-production groups.
_Avoid_: Zone, workshop as a generic label for every unit.

**Zone** (Ukrainian: Зона; Russian: Зона):
A specific place of responsibility belonging to one unit. Shift assignments, workplace handover
and photo inspection identify a zone. Photo rules belong to a checklist and a particular zone.
_Avoid_: Unit, department, subdivision.
