# Connecteam: adjacent competitors and market-leadership evidence

Research date: 2026-09-12. Scope: public-source competitive orientation, not a procurement recommendation or a market-share study. Vendor capabilities below are published claims, not hands-on verification. Product availability and payroll coverage require regional verification before use in Ukraine.

## Market-leadership conclusion

No comparable, independent market-share measurement establishing Connecteam as the global number-one vendor was found in this bounded search. The category overlaps workforce scheduling, time and attendance, employee communications, HR, and frontline operations; leadership would need a defined category, geography, date, and metric.

[Gartner Peer Insights](https://www.gartner.com/reviews/product/connecteam) lists Connecteam in workforce-management and employee-communications categories. Its review ratings are user opinions, not market share or a Gartner vendor endorsement. Vendor customer counts, review scores, awards, and "number one" slogans should not be treated as proof of global leadership.

The distinctions in the last column are research interpretations of the published feature emphasis, not claims that other capabilities are impossible or unavailable.

## Evidence and limitations

- Deputy, When I Work, Homebase and Microsoft documentation were retrieved directly during this run.
- SafetyCulture direct page retrieval returned HTTP 429. Its official platform page was available through search-index content, which reported an older crawl. The feature summary is supported by that official source, but current packaging and detailed availability were not confirmed live.
- No competitor accounts were created and no products were tested. No numerical competitor ranking or prices are asserted.
- The useful next comparison is a real production journey: attendance, work/break transitions, checks and photo evidence, incident handling, handover and closure. A feature-name checklist alone cannot establish workflow fit.
