Research date: 2026-09-12. Sources are vendor-owned pages. Prices describe subscriptions, not custom software development, implementation, ownership transfer, or resale rights. Confirm taxes, regional currency, seat eligibility, and checkout terms before purchase. No purchase or vendor contact was made.

## What this evidence establishes

These are overlapping categories, not verified exact substitutes. Connecteam combines workforce administration and daily operations; Jibble focuses on time and attendance; MaintainX focuses on maintenance workflows. The published capabilities do not establish equivalence to a specific Telegram-led process, QR flow, shift-handover rule set, localization, or operational support agreement.

The existence of competing offers establishes that vendors actively supply related solutions, including low-cost alternatives. Pricing pages alone do not establish paid adoption, willingness to pay among Ukrainian manufacturers, or demand for a particular new product. None of those claims was independently measured here.

Subscription prices are useful as buyer alternatives and budget context. They cannot directly price the work of designing and maintaining a bespoke system, nor the commercial rights to it. A meaningful comparison requires the actual number and roles of users, required workflows, implementation effort, integrations, support obligations, and demonstrable operating benefits.
