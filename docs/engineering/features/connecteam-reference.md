## Lean recommendation

**Simplify / Proceed to bounded validation**: remove duplicate reporting and manual follow-up by
linking findings to accountable work, placing short instructions in context and showing who must act.
Prioritize reliability before a general automation builder. Trial qualifications/onboarding only against
real role requirements and named assessor authority. Defer duplicate messengers/social features.

Measures to collect: unresolved action age, master follow-up effort, instruction retrieval time,
repeat questions, failed/unacknowledged notices and overdue renewal evidence. No savings are measured
here. Worker interaction time and shift intervals must not be represented as equipment downtime/OEE.
