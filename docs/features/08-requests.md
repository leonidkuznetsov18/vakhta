# Requests (spec 8)

In the bot under "Обращения": late arrival, early leave, shift swap, day off, vacation, sick
leave (with a medical document photo, stored privately), appeal of a score, event correction
("Коррекция события"). Each request has a route: who decides (master, unit manager, HR) and a
deadline. A swap needs the counterpart's consent in the bot first.

Panel "Обращения": inbox of pending requests, details, decision with a comment; approved
corrections rewrite intervals through a correction event, an approved swap creates a new schedule
version. Potential overtime waits for approval in the same section.

The employee gets a bot message with the decision. Open requests keep the bonus criterion
"no unresolved items" pending until decided.
