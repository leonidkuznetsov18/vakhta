## Platform

web

## Users and purpose

Vakhta's panel serves shift masters, production managers and supporting administrative roles in
continuous production. Canonical product truth: ../../docs/product-vision.md. Workers use Telegram.

## Confirmed communication scope

The owner requested individual and bulk messages, media attachments, multi-question questionnaires
with free-text responses, global quick access and mobile support. Ordinary two-way chat is excluded.
The communication task must preserve operational context and replace the modal interaction.

## Constraints

Use the existing Vite/React panel, three locales and scoped permissions. Keep human responsibility,
immutable operational history and worker shift flows. Accessibility and mobile operation are required.
Feature contract: ../../specs/006-employee-communications/spec.md. Proposed design is feature-scoped;
no global rebrand, unverified production claim or adoption metric is implied.
